Listenoop Privacy Policy

Listenoop processes audio on your device to detect clips and play them back. When you import audio, Listenoop copies the files into its app storage. It also stores playlists, settings, and playback position there so you can pick up where you left off.

Listenoop does not require an account, upload your audio or playback data to its own servers, or use advertising or analytics SDKs. Your device may include app data in system backups or transfers, depending on your device settings.

Removing a track or playlist removes its listing. The imported audio copy can remain in app storage. To erase all local Listenoop data, delete the app on iOS or clear its storage on Android. Manage existing backups separately in your device or backup account settings.
